# Your label definitions, one per line as `label: definition`.
#
# Copied from the README Label Taxonomy so the baseline is judged on exactly
# the definitions written there. baseline.py reads this file.

beginner_recommendation: suggests which plants (or how many) a new owner should start with
soil_repotting: about potting mix, pot size, drainage, or when and how to repot
watering: about how much, how often, or by what method to water, including over- and underwatering
general_care: routine upkeep that isn't one specific topic: inspections, cleaning, fertilizing, seasonal adjustments, and the general habit or mindset of looking after plants
pest_management: about spotting, preventing or treating pests, including quarantining new plants
light_placement: about how much light a plant gets and where to put it
pet_safety: about whether plants are toxic or safe around cats, dogs and other pets
propagation: about making new plants from an existing one: cuttings, water rooting, division, offsets
